Financial analysts say more Tanzanians in urban centres are turning to mobile-based savings and investment platforms as a way of building financial discipline outside traditional banking channels. The trend, driven largely by young professionals and small business owners, has seen increased uptake of apps that allow users to save small amounts daily while tracking their progress toward specific goals. Experts note that this shift is gradually reshaping personal finance habits, encouraging a culture of saving even among low-income earners who previously found formal banking services inaccessible or costly to maintain.
