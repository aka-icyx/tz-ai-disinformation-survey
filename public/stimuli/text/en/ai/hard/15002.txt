Grassroots football academies across Dar es Salaam are increasingly attracting attention from talent scouts, with several young players securing trials at clubs in neighbouring countries this year. Coaches say the growing visibility stems from improved training facilities and regular participation in regional youth tournaments.

Parents and local sports administrators note that this progress has encouraged more families to support their children's athletic ambitions, viewing football as a viable pathway alongside education. Sports analysts believe sustained investment in youth development could position Tanzania as a stronger source of talent within East African football circles.
