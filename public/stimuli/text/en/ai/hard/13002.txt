More smallholder farmers in the southern highlands are turning to greenhouse farming to extend growing seasons and protect crops from unpredictable weather patterns. Agricultural officers say the shift has been particularly beneficial for vegetable growers, who report higher yields and reduced losses compared to traditional open-field methods.

Local cooperatives have begun offering shared greenhouse facilities to farmers who cannot afford individual setups, allowing more households to access the technology. Extension workers note that this collective approach is gradually building confidence among smallholders eager to diversify beyond staple crops into more profitable horticultural produce.
