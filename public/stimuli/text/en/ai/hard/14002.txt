Insurance providers are reporting growing interest among small business owners in coverage against fire and theft, particularly in busy commercial districts where such incidents have historically disrupted operations. Agents note that many entrepreneurs are now factoring insurance costs into their annual budgets rather than treating it as an afterthought.

Industry representatives say this shift reflects a broader change in risk awareness among traders, who increasingly view coverage as a practical safeguard for protecting inventory and equipment, especially as competition intensifies and the financial impact of unexpected losses becomes harder for small enterprises to absorb.
