More families in fast-growing suburban areas are seeking formal land titles as a way to secure their properties against future disputes, particularly as land values continue to rise near expanding urban centres. Surveyors report a steady increase in applications from residents who previously relied on informal purchase agreements passed down through word of mouth.

Legal experts say the trend reflects growing awareness among landowners about the risks of undocumented transactions, with many now prioritizing proper registration despite the additional costs, viewing it as a necessary safeguard for protecting family investments over the long term.
