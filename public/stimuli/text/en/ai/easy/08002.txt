More small retailers across Dar es Salaam are adopting inventory management apps to track stock levels and sales in real time, moving away from handwritten ledgers that often led to costly errors. Business owners say the shift has made it easier to identify best-selling products and plan restocking more efficiently.

Tech vendors offering these tools note growing interest from shop owners in secondary cities as well, suggesting the trend is gradually spreading beyond major urban centres as more entrepreneurs recognise the value of digital record-keeping in running leaner, more profitable operations.
