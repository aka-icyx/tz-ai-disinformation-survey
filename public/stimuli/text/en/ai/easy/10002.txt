Community Wi-Fi hotspots are emerging as a practical alternative for residents in peri-urban areas struggling with the cost of individual mobile data plans, with local entrepreneurs setting up shared connection points charging small hourly fees. Users say this arrangement allows them to access essential services, from mobile banking to online learning, without depleting household budgets.

Some operators have begun bundling hotspot access with charging stations and printing services, creating small digital hubs that serve as informal community centres. Residents note these spaces have become especially valuable for students and job seekers who previously had limited affordable internet access.
