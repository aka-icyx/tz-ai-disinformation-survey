Fibre optic expansion into secondary towns is beginning to reshape how local businesses operate, with cyber cafés and small offices reporting faster, more reliable connections than the mobile data they previously depended on. Owners say the shift has reduced downtime during peak hours, allowing them to serve more customers without interruption.

Telecom technicians working on these projects note that demand for fixed-line connections is rising steadily among small enterprises seeking stability for services such as video calls, cloud-based transactions and online applications, signalling a gradual but noticeable shift away from reliance on mobile-only internet access.
