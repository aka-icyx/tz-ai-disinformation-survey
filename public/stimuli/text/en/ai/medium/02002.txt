A growing number of Tanzanian entrepreneurs are turning their attention to the logistics and warehousing sector, viewing it as a promising avenue for regional trade expansion. Industry players say demand for modern storage facilities has risen steadily as more businesses seek efficient ways to move goods across East Africa. One local investor noted that partnerships with international firms have become increasingly common, allowing smaller companies to access better equipment and expertise. Analysts believe this collaborative approach could strengthen Tanzania's position as a key trade hub within the region in the coming years.
