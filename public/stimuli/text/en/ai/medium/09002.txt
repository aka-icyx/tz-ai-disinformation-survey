Startups in Tanzania's fintech space are increasingly citing inconsistent regulatory guidance as a barrier to scaling their operations, with some founders noting that similar products face different levels of scrutiny depending on which agency reviews them. This unpredictability, they argue, makes it difficult to plan long-term investments confidently.

Industry analysts suggest that clearer, standardized frameworks could encourage more innovation by giving entrepreneurs a stable foundation to build on, rather than navigating a patchwork of interpretations that often varies from one review process to another, discouraging smaller players from entering the market altogether.
