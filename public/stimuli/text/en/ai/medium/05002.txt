Kigoma Region is drawing renewed attention from renewable energy investors eyeing its potential for small-scale hydropower development along tributaries feeding Lake Tanganyika. Preliminary assessments suggest several sites could support mini-grid systems capable of serving surrounding villages currently reliant on costly diesel generators or firewood for daily energy needs.

Energy experts say such localized projects could offer a faster, more affordable path to electrification than extending the national grid to remote areas, while also reducing environmental pressure caused by continued deforestation for fuel in the region.
