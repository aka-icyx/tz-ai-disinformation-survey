Simbu and Shauri have established themselves among Tanzania’s leading marathoners, while Fernandez is an emerging athlete looking to make his mark on the international stage.

The TCS Sydney Marathon is a World Athletics Platinum Label event and is the sole remaining participant legacy event of the Sydney 2000 Olympic Games.

The marathon course takes runners across the iconic Sydney Harbour Bridge and past notable landmarks, including Circular Quay and Centennial Park, before finishing in front of the world-famous Sydney Opera House.
