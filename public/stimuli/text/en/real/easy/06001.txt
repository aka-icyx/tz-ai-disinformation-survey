When a private company finances a PPP, it generally raises that capital from banks and other financial institutions. In many cases, the private company will borrow at a higher cost than the government could borrow directly. Investors also expect a return on their capital, which ultimately has to come from somewhere—usually government payments, user fees, tariffs, or a combination of these.

This is particularly important for a country such as Tanzania, where the affordability of essential services is a major concern.
